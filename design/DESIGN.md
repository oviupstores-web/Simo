## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a fresh, tactile, and modern food-tech experience tailored for intelligent meal planning, recipe discovery, and seamless grocery management. The brand radiates warmth, culinary optimism, healthfulness, and clarity.

The visual direction blends **Tactile Soft Modernism** with clean, functional **Minimalism**:
- **Target Audience:** Busy individuals, health-conscious eaters, families, and fitness enthusiasts seeking effortless, nutritionally balanced lifestyle routines without cognitive overload.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspiring, organic, revitalizing, approachable, and trustworthy. The interface feels as crisp and inviting as freshly harvested produce while functioning with precision and intelligent clarity.
- **Visual Personality:** Soft organic contours, pill-shaped call-to-actions, wholesome natural tones, and crisp editorial typography that champions high-contrast dark botanical greens over sterile corporate grays.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from wholesome harvest ingredients—crisp carrots, nutrient-dense leafy greens, and sunlit soil:

- **Primary (`#F5821F`):** A radiant citrus orange used for high-intent conversion points, primary call-to-actions, key interactive states, and motivational milestones.
- **Secondary (`#6BAE3F`):** A vital leaf green dedicated to nutritional progress, positive diet indicators, eco-friendly badges, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#1E4620`):** A rich, grounded forest green used for high-contrast typographic titles, primary headers, dark navigation bars, and anchored brand statements.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAFAF8`):** An organic off-white background with a delicate warm undertone, avoiding stark digital glare.
- **Surface / Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white elevated planes paired with subtle warm shadows to give cards natural physical separation against the canvas.
- **Neutral Dark / Body Text (`#2D3748`):** Deep charcoal slate engineered for continuous reading legibility across recipe cards and ingredient breakdowns.
- **Muted Borders & Dividers (`#EAECE5`):** Soft, organic divider lines that maintain structure without rigid visual noise.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** serves as the unified typographic voice across all touchpoints. Its geometric base and sculpted, rounded terminals strike an optimal balance between friendly approachability and clinical nutritional clarity:

- **Headlines:** Set in Bold (`700`) and ExtraBold (`800`) utilizing `#1E4620` to project organic authority and premium warmth. Tight tracking creates punchy, confident titles for meals and dietary trackers.
- **Body:** Set in Medium (`500`) and Regular (`400`) in slate charcoal (`#2D3748`) to ensure effortless legibility when scanning recipe instructions and ingredient lists.
- **Labels & Badges:** Set in Semibold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) with proportional letter spacing for macronutrient callouts, dietary filter tags (e.g., Vegan, Keto), and preparation times.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized primarily for a compact, ergonomic mobile frame (390px - 430px) adhering to fluid single-column and multi-card modular grids:

- **Mobile Viewports (390px - 430px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins, accommodating comfortable thumb reach.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Built around an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for tight badge and micro-metric alignments. Component internal padding scales smoothly between `space-sm` (8px) for micro-metrics and `space-lg` (20px) for prominent meal cards.
- **Safe Areas & Fixed Bottom Bars:** Floating bottom navigation, meal logger buttons, and grocery cart CTA strips adhere to a 16px bottom offset above hardware home indicators to maintain thumb ergonomics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **warm ambient shadows and tactile surface stacking** rather than stark drop shadows, giving surfaces a soft, physical feel:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Grounded at `#FAFAF8`. Flat and quiet.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces layered with an ambient, warm diffused shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(30, 70, 32, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Bottom Sheets):** Elevated overlays utilizing `0px 12px 32px rgba(30, 70, 32, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Primary Action Depth:** Orange CTA buttons feature a subtle, matched warm cast shadow (`0px 8px 20px rgba(245, 130, 31, 0.28)`) that produces a buoyant, tappable appearance.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates organic, soft curvature inspired by whole natural foods:

- **Standard Cards & Containers:** Standardized with `1rem` to `1.25rem` (16px - 20px) corner radius, softening screen density and inviting tactile exploration.
- **Buttons, Tags & Input Fields:** Styled as complete pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px) or high-radius geometries (`rounded-2xl` / 16px) for an ergonomic, friendly hand-held feel.
- **Food Imagery & Visual Masks:** Meal photos, grocery thumbnails, and profile cards utilize `rounded-2xl` to harmonize seamlessly with enclosing cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Call to Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Full-width pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) vibrant orange button (`#F5821F`) with white semibold text, styled with a warm glow shadow (`0px 8px 20px rgba(245, 130, 31, 0.28)`). On active tap: scales down smoothly to `0.98` with darkened fill (`#DC7216`).
- **Secondary Action:** Pill button in light meadow tint (`rgba(107, 174, 63, 0.12)`) featuring bold secondary green text (`#6BAE3F`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Transparent background with `#1E4620` text, highlighting chevron or back-navigation icons.

### Meal & Nutrition Cards
- Structured in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with `1.25rem` (20px) border-radius and Level 1 elevation.
- Includes upper image presentation with organic top radii, embedded dietary tag chips, prep-time metadata badges, and macro breakdowns (Protein, Carbs, Fat) rendered in clear numerical hierarchy.

### Dietary & Filter Chips
- Capsule-shaped pills with `0.5rem` vertical and `0.875rem` horizontal padding.
- **Inactive State:** Background `#F2F3EE`, text `#2D3748`.
- **Active State:** Solid `#1E4620` background with white text or lively `#6BAE3F` fill with crisp white text.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Rounded pill search bars (`rounded-full`) with `#FFFFFF` background, a faint perimeter border (`#EAECE5`), left-aligned search icon in dark green, and placeholder text in warm slate gray.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in primary orange (`#F5821F`) without heavy shadow.

### Checkboxes & Grocery Lists
- Rounded-circle checkboxes (`rounded-full`) instead of square boxes.
- **Unchecked:** 2px border in `#D4D8CE`.
- **Checked:** Filled with vibrant green (`#6BAE3F`) carrying a crisp white checkmark, accompanied by clean strike-through and dimmed text styling for checked grocery items.

### Floating Bottom Navigation Bar
- Frosted white glass substrate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), featuring an elevated circular central AI Meal Planner button wrapped in `#F5821F` with Level 2 elevation.
